THE MISSION IS NEVER OVER.

Dustoff! is a fast-paced side-scrolling rescue game inspired by the golden age of arcade action. Pilot your helicopter behind enemy lines, dodge tank fire and enemy jets, and evacuate hostages before it's too late.

GAMEPLAY
━━━━━━━━
• Fly freely across hostile territory in intense side-scrolling action
• Rescue trapped civilians from five brutal environments: scorched desert, dense jungle, frozen arctic, tropical island, and an alien world
• Survive enemy tanks, jet fighters, and alien saucers that escalate in intensity each sortie
• Manage fuel carefully — run dry and the mission fails
• Land, board, and deliver hostages to base before the enemy overruns the field

FEATURES
━━━━━━━━
• Three visual modes: original pixel-art retro, modern pixel art, and hi-res painterly
• Dynamic music system — calm, tension, and action layers shift with the threat level
• Five handcrafted biomes, each with unique enemies, scenery, and atmosphere
• Touch controls optimised for mobile: floating virtual joystick, tap-to-fire, dedicated face button
• Controller support (Bluetooth gamepads)
• Runs on Android and in the browser — play anywhere

A GAME BUILT WITH CARE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Dustoff! is a love letter to the rescue helicopter games of the early 1980s, rebuilt from scratch with modern tools for modern hardware. The mechanics are faithful to the classics — momentum-based flight, punishing enemies, tense rescues — with new visual flair and five times the content.

No ads. No in-app purchases. Just one great game.
